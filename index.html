About ShaqoHel

Hal meel oo aad ka bilaabi karto safarkaaga shaqo.

ShaqoHel waa platform loogu talagalay in shaqo-doonka Soomaaliyeed uu si fudud u helo fursadaha shaqo ee la daabacay. Waxaan diiradda saarnaa in shaqooyinka lagu soo bandhigo si nadiif ah, xogtoodana si cad loo akhrisan karo.

Shaqo-doonku wuxuu raadin karaa shaqo, company, location ama category. Sidoo kale qof ama shirkad leh fursad shaqo waxay isticmaali kartaa Add Job, kadibna shaqadu waxay martaa hubin iyo approval ka hor inta aysan public-ka kasoo muuqan.

Our Purpose

How It Works

Sida ShaqoHel loo isticmaalo

Saddex tallaabo oo fudud ayaa kuu fududaynaya inaad hesho ama aad soo gudbiso fursad shaqo.

01

Raadi

Ku qor magaca shaqada, company-ga ama location-ka aad rabto inaad ka raadiso.

02

Dooro

Fur job-ka aad xiisaynayso oo akhri description-ka, requirements-ka iyo faahfaahinta kale.

03

Apply

Riix Apply Now haddii shaqadu leedahay Application URL, kadibna raac habka codsiga ee loo diyaariyey.

Ma haysaa fursad shaqo?
